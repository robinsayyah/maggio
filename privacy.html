Privacy Policy

Maggio helps you manage events, friends, messages, and calendar-based availability. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used to provide those features.

Last update: 01 July 2026
1

Overview

Maggio helps you manage events, friends, messages, and calendar-based availability. We collect and use only the information needed to provide those features.

2

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, profile information, friend connections, event details, messages, and calendar availability that you choose to sync. If you allow notifications, we also use Apple push notification services to deliver alerts.

3

How We Use Information

We use your information to sign you in, create your profile, show your availability, let you send and receive messages, manage event invitations and RSVPs, and deliver notifications related to app activity.

4

Sharing

Information is shared only as needed for app features. Other Maggio users may see your display name, event participation, messages you send, and availability information you choose to make visible through the app. We do not sell your personal information.

5

Storage

Maggio stores app data using Apple services, including CloudKit and Sign in with Apple. Calendar access and notifications are handled through Apple system frameworks and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

6

Retention and Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active so the app can function. If you want your account or app data deleted, use the support contact provided for the app. Signing out of the app does not automatically delete stored data.

7

Contact Us

For privacy questions or account-data requests, contact hej@rpsstudio.se.
